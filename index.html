Porthole

Windows PC games, in your pocket.

Porthole runs Windows games on your iPhone and iPad. Bring your own game files, download the games you own on Steam, and see how each one runs on devices like yours before you try it.

Early hobby project. Porthole isn't on the App Store and there's nothing to download yet. It changes often, and plenty of games won't run.

What it does

Add games your way

Import a game folder from the Files app, or run a Windows installer. Porthole finds the right program in the folder for you, and keeps uninstallers and helper tools out of the way.

Your Steam library

Sign in and download the Windows games you own. They start through Steam itself, so your licence is checked the normal way.

Compatibility reports

Each game shows a badge: works great, works with problems, or doesn't work, based on reports from other people. See how it runs on your model, and add your own report in under a minute.

Made for iPhone and iPad

Touch controls you can switch while you play, a page for every game with its own performance settings, and a layout that uses the whole iPad screen.

No ads, no account

Porthole has no ads and no account of its own. A compatibility report is only sent when you choose to send one, and it contains your device model, iOS version and your answers, nothing that names you.

How it works

Porthole is built on Madeira, a project that runs Windows programs on iOS by combining Wine, the FEX-Emu translator and DXMT, which turns Direct3D into Metal. Games run through that compatibility layer, so speed depends on the game and on the device. Some games need more memory than a phone has.

It needs iOS 18 or later and JIT switched on, which Porthole can do by itself once you give it a pairing file (on iOS 27 it can make one on the device).
