What's new

What's new in Porthole

Porthole is in early development; 1.0.0 is its first public release. This is what has changed in the builds so far, in plain words, newest first.

Stores and downloads

  • One Stores tab. Steam, Epic Games, GOG, itch.io and an All games page share it, with a switcher at the top. Every game carries the logo of the store it came from, on its card and on the home screen.
  • Epic Games. Sign in on Epic's own page, then install the games your account owns that have a Windows version and play them without Epic's launcher. Epic's overlay and anti-cheat are not supported.
  • GOG. Sign in on GOG's own page and install the games you own, straight from GOG's servers where it has a build there, otherwise from GOG's setup file. Games that need GOG Galaxy's online features are not supported.
  • itch.io. Paste a personal API key made on itch.io and install the games you bought, claimed or collected, including free ones.
  • The same layout for every store: a grid of your games with a badge for what each is doing and a page for every game, with its compatibility badge, description and screenshots where the store has them.
  • One Downloads page for every store, opened by a round button that stays at the bottom right of the Stores tab. It shows what each store is downloading, the games waiting and the paused ones, with Pause, Resume, Download now, Pause all and Resume all.
  • Downloads keep going when you leave the app, and a card on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island shows the percentage, size, speed and time left with Pause, Resume and Cancel buttons. The card is an optional part of the install; without it iOS's own progress card is used.
  • Steam games on an external drive. Choose a folder on an SSD and send new Steam downloads there. This is new and has had little testing.
  • Much less lag in the Stores tab and while something downloads, and swiping in from the left edge goes back, as in any iOS app.

Security and privacy

  • Store sign-ins stay in the iOS Keychain, for this device only. Store traffic is kept out of the network cache, and sign-in pages keep no cookies.
  • Pairing files, certificates and profiles you pick are deleted from temporary storage as soon as they are read.
  • Updates are checked against the maintainer's signature before they are installed.
  • Problem reports also hide IPv6 addresses and any password, token or key that appears next to its name, and the pairing log no longer names your iPhone.

Updating the app

  • A popup while it updates itself with the steps (download, sign, install), one progress bar, and a plain warning to keep the app open until it is done.
  • Signing is several times faster, and so is sending the signed app to iOS's installer.

The engine

  • Ten open fixes from the Madeira project were taken in, among them the reduced-precision x87 switch working as its name says, a large-memory option for games that reserve a lot at start, more of Windows' own DirectX helper libraries, and fixes for launchers that start a second copy of themselves.

Look and feel

  • A new home screen. Your games sit on one shelf. With a controller connected the chosen game grows and the rest step back; on touch every game is the same size.
  • A slim top bar on iPhone and iPad, with the Porthole mark and the name next to it.
  • A game's page is short again: Play, three settings, and four doors (controls, how it runs, more options, about and files).
  • An app icon: a silver porthole looking at a sunset sea, and a night sky with a crescent moon in dark mode.
  • Dark only, in a slate blue in the Steam spirit with Porthole's own cyan.

Controllers

  • FULL and PARTIAL badges from Steam's own list, on the shelf, the Steam screen and every game's page.
  • Press the badge to see what it means and how to cover the parts a controller can't reach.
  • A Controller tab on the Steam screen that lists only the games with controller support.

Steam

  • Add an installed game back to your library at any time; it still starts through Steam.
  • A clearer Steam screen and Downloads page, with labelled buttons and one summary line.

Compatibility reports

  • Shorter lists, with the details one switch away.
  • Reports are counted per device model and per engine version, so an old result isn't passed off as a current one.

Setup and updates

  • Setup is three steps: full speed, Windows components, your games from any of the four stores, then a short Good to know page.
  • A warning in the game when memory or the swap file is nearly full, saying what to change.
  • Settings has six pages instead of fifteen.
  • Updates: versions are listed five at a time; with no certificate added you can download any version as a file.
  • Your VPN: Porthole asks before pausing it, and can turn it back on with a shortcut you make.
  • Notices on the home screen can be hidden with a small x.

Every build, newest first

The last 25 builds, exactly as the app lists them in Settings › Updates. This list is made from the project's change log.

1.1.0 (build 551): Full Steam Ahead: much more Steam (versions, DLC, achievements, friends, the Workshop), Amazon Games, Restart game, and fixes for American Truck Simulator, Megabonk and Counter-Strike 2.

Added

  • Amazon Games in the Stores tab: sign in on Amazon's own page and install the games you bought or claimed with Prime Gaming. Files come straight from Amazon's servers and are checked against Amazon's signed file list.
  • Steam: a Version and DLC row on a game's page. Pick another version Steam offers (a beta or the previous version), type a beta password for a private beta, and turn DLC you own on or off. Only the parts that differ are downloaded.
  • Steam: achievements with pictures, descriptions, unlock dates and how many players have them, a progress line ("12 of 50 unlocked") on the game's page, pop-ups when you unlock one in a game, and the full list in the in-game menu (Steam > Achievements).
  • Steam: your friends (read only): who is playing what, who is online, and who was last seen when, on a Friends button at the top right of the Home screen, in the Steam list and in the in-game menu, plus a Friends playing row on a game's page. Nothing is sent to Porthole's servers.
  • Steam: news and patch notes for a game, and Steam's review score and Steam Deck rating on its page (public Steam pages, no sign-in).
  • Steam Workshop (beta): browse, search, subscribe to and download a game's items in the app and inside the running game (in-game menu > Steam). Items are checked for updates when a game's page opens (switch it off with Update mods automatically). Depot items such as American Truck Simulator mods can be subscribed to while a game runs and download when it closes. Tested with Portal 2 and American Truck Simulator.
  • Restart game, in the in-game menu and on the Workshop page: closes the game and Porthole, then starts the same game again when you open Porthole (iOS needs one tap on a notification). If a game ignores the close request, Porthole asks whether to stop it.
  • A Steam menu in the running game opens when a game asks for Steam's overlay (its own Workshop or Store button), instead of leaving the game stuck until you press Escape.
  • Your own VPN can come back as soon as JIT is on (Settings > JIT and tunnel), for networks that block Steam.
  • The launch screen says why Steam will not start a game (needs an update, or files marked damaged) and what to do, and says when Steam has not signed in.
  • Add a game > Import a game folder says how to choose the folder, and writes what happens to the log.

Changed

  • Signing in to Steam starts with the QR code (approved in the Steam mobile app, so Porthole never handles your password). The sign-in screen says Porthole is unofficial and not affiliated with Valve.
  • Verify and update files on a Steam game's page really checks every file against Steam now and downloads only what is missing or damaged. Updating a game also keeps the parts of files already on the device that have not changed.
  • Megabonk starts without a hand-typed engine line and its controller works in Automatic. Sorting the all-games page by recently played now includes GOG and itch.io games.
  • Engine r17 (it was r13 in 1.0.1). Compatibility badges start empty for it.

Fixed

  • American Truck Simulator (and other games that create a tiny placeholder window and rely on DirectX to size it) showed as a postage stamp. The window is now resized or made to cover the screen.
  • Megabonk, and any Unity game that uses Rewired's Windows.Gaming.Input plugin, saw no controller. The controller is now also offered as a Windows.Gaming.Input gamepad for these games.
  • Counter-Strike 2's first-person look stopped at a fixed angle. The game's mouse-input thread needs a system window class that was missing for the game, so it never got raw mouse movement.
  • Portal 2 and other single-file Workshop items are recorded the way Steam records them and put where Steam keeps finished downloads, so the game opens maps the app downloaded even where Steam's own download times out.
  • A Steam game is no longer started with too little memory room: on a bad start of the app the room for the game's translated code can shrink and the game runs out of memory a minute in. Porthole now stops, says so and offers Close Porthole.
  • Workshop single-file items downloaded from a web address (Portal 2 maps) no longer fail with "Steam did not accept the sign-in token".
1.1.50 (build 550): Counter-Strike 2 look: the missing Message window class (engine r17).

Changed

  • Engine r17 (badges start empty for it).

Fixed

  • Counter-Strike 2's first-person look stopped at a fixed angle in every control layout. The game's mouse-input thread needs a small hidden window of the system class "Message", and that class was only registered for the first program Porthole ran, not for the game started by Steam's client, so the window could not be created and the game never received raw mouse movement (it fell back to the pointer position, which stops at the screen edge). The Message class is now registered for every program. Needs a test on a device.
1.1.49 (build 549): A Steam game is not started with too little memory room.

Fixed

  • Counter-Strike 2 and other Steam games failed a minute after starting with "the system ran out of memory" on some starts of the app. iOS places memory differently on every start of Porthole, and on a bad one it had put something where Porthole keeps the room for the game's translated code, so that room shrank (544 MB instead of about 900 MB) and ran out as the game loaded. Porthole now sees the small room before the game starts, stops, says so, and offers Close Porthole: open it again and start the game (it is different every time).
1.1.48 (build 548): Verify and update files only downloads what is missing or changed.

Changed

  • "Verify and update files" on a Steam game's page now really checks every file against Steam (a few minutes for a big game) and downloads only the parts that are missing or damaged. Before, it never looked at the files: for a game that was up to date it did nothing, and for a game with a newer build it downloaded every file of the changed depot again.

Fixed

  • Updating a Steam game (a newer build, a beta, DLC) no longer downloads files that are already on the device and unchanged: the parts of each file already on the disk are compared with Steam's list and kept.
1.1.47 (build 547): Launch screen says why Steam will not start a game.

Fixed

  • When Valve's Steam client refuses to start a game because its files are out of date or marked damaged (it cannot download in the game), the launch screen stayed on "Signing in to Steam". It now says "Steam won't start the game yet" and what to do: update the game under Stores > Steam (when a newer build is on Steam), or use Verify files under Details and files.
1.1.46 (build 546): Counter-Strike 2 look: more diagnostics.

Added

  • A log line that names the window class a thread could not create (for Counter-Strike 2's mouse-input thread). Nothing else changes.
1.1.45 (build 545): Counter-Strike 2 look: fix attempt (engine r16).

Changed

  • Engine r16 (badges start empty for it).

Fixed

  • Counter-Strike 2's first-person look stopped at a fixed angle, in every control layout. The game's mouse-input thread is started late and could not create the small hidden window it needs (no usable desktop), so the game never received raw mouse movement and fell back to the pointer position, which stops at the screen edge. A thread with no usable desktop now gets the process's desktop, so the window is created and raw mouse input is registered. The steps are written to the log as [desktop-diag]. Needs a test on a device.
1.1.44 (build 544): Workshop maps recorded the way Steam records them; window-creation diagnostics.

Added

  • Log lines ([createwin] 0159) that show why a window cannot be created, to find why Counter-Strike 2's look stops at a fixed angle (its raw-input window is created and the thread ends at once). Nothing else about windows changes.

Fixed

  • Single-file Workshop items (Portal 2 and Counter-Strike maps) are now recorded the way Steam's client records them (manifest -1 and the item's UGC handle). Before, Steam's client saw them as changed, marked the game "needs update", tried to download them itself and cleaned away the copy the app had put in its finished-download cache, so the game said the map was not downloaded. Items downloaded earlier are corrected when the app opens and when a game's page opens.
1.1.43 (build 543): Counter-Strike 2 look: diagnostics.

Added

  • Log lines (look-diag) that show what a game registers for mouse input and how the first-person look reaches it, to find why Counter-Strike 2's look stops at a fixed angle. Nothing else changes.
1.1.42 (build 542): Portal 2 Workshop maps open when Steam's client can't download them itself.

Fixed

  • Workshop maps that are a single file (Portal 2) did not open on a connection where Steam's client inside the game times out reaching the content server, even though the app had downloaded them. The app now also puts each map where Steam's client keeps a finished download (userdata/<account>/ugc/referenced/<id>/, with the data.vdf file Steam writes), so the game finds it. Maps already downloaded are filled in when the app opens and when a game's page opens. Needs Steam to be signed in; nothing is sent anywhere new.
1.1.41 (build 541): Restart game when a game ignores the close request.

Fixed

  • Restart game did nothing with a game that ignores the close request (Alt+F4), such as Portal 2: the game kept running and Porthole waited for it. It now sends the request twice, and if the game has not closed after 8 seconds asks whether to stop it and restart (Keep playing / Stop and restart). The steps are written to the log as [restart].
1.1.40 (build 540): Importing a game folder is explained and logged.

Added

  • The steps of importing a game folder (the picker opened or closed, the folder chosen, how many files, errors) are now written to the log, so a report about a folder that "won't select" shows what happened.

Changed

  • Add a game > Import a game folder now says how to choose the folder: in Files, open the game's folder, then tap Open at the top right. Tapping a folder only goes into it; Open chooses the folder you are in.
1.1.39 (build 539): Achievement progress on the game pages.

Added

  • The Achievements row on a Steam game's page (in the Stores tab and in the library) now says how many you have, for example "12 of 50 unlocked" or "All 50 unlocked", without opening the list. It is read when the page opens (at most once an hour per game) and kept on the device only; signing out forgets it.
1.1.38 (build 538): News and Deck rating on Steam store pages.

Added

  • The News and patch notes row and the Steam Deck rating chip are now also on a game's page in the Stores tab (the page you see before installing), not only on installed games' pages in the library.
1.1.37 (build 537): Steam news, review score and Deck rating.

Added

  • A News and patch notes row on a Steam game's page: the game's official announcements and patch notes from Steam, newest first, with a link to read each on Steam. The same list is in the in-game menu (Steam > News), where it only reads.
  • Two chips on a Steam game's page: Steam's review summary (for example Overwhelmingly Positive, 99%) and its Steam Deck rating (Verified, Playable or Unsupported). The Deck rating is Valve's test on a Steam Deck, not a promise about how the game runs in Porthole.
  • These come from Steam's public pages. No sign-in is used and only the game's number is sent.
1.1.36 (build 536): Restart game.

Added

  • Restart game, in the in-game menu (Session) and on the Workshop page when a mod has just arrived or is waiting for the game to close. It closes the game the usual way (so it can save), closes Porthole, and sends a notification: tap it, or open Porthole yourself within 30 minutes, and the same game starts again after the Workshop downloads that were waiting have finished (up to two minutes). A notice on the Home screen shows it and has a Cancel button. iOS does not let an app reopen itself, which is why the notification is needed.
1.1.35 (build 535): Workshop updates are found and downloaded by themselves.

Added

  • When you open a Steam game's page, Porthole checks whether any Workshop item you are subscribed to is missing or has a newer version on Steam, and downloads it (at most every 5 minutes per game, and not while a game is running). The Workshop row on the game page says "N updates available" when it is only counting. A new switch on the Workshop page, Update mods automatically, turns the automatic download off; the button below it still updates on demand.

Changed

  • Opening a game's Workshop page only downloads missing and updated items by itself when Update mods automatically is on.
1.1.34 (build 534): Friends playing this game.

Added

  • On a Steam game's page, a Friends playing row shows which of your Steam friends are in that game right now (for example "2 playing now"), and opens the list of who they are. Read only, from the friends list the app already loads.
1.1.33 (build 533): Friends button on the Home Screen.

Added

  • A Friends button at the top right of the Home Screen, always visible there (and only there, not on a game's page). It opens your Steam friends list and shows how many are online once the list has loaded. If you are not signed in to Steam it offers to open Steam to sign in.
1.1.32 (build 532): Steam friends list (test build).

Added

  • Your Steam friends, read only: who is playing what, who is online, and (folded away) who is offline, with their picture and how long ago they were last seen. It is on the Steam game list (the Friends button next to Sort) and in the in-game menu (Steam > Friends). It refreshes every 45 seconds while it is open. Names, pictures and status come from Steam with the sign-in the app already has; nothing is sent to Porthole's servers and the list is kept in memory only. Ignored, blocked and pending people are not shown.

Changed

  • The friends probe from the last two test builds is gone.
1.1.31 (build 531): Friends list probe, second try (log lines only, test build).

Changed

  • Test build: the friends probe now reads the ids the way Steam writes them. Log lines only.
1.1.30 (build 530): Friends list probe (log lines only, test build).

Changed

  • Test build for the friends list. Twenty seconds after the app opens (when you are signed in to Steam) it asks Steam three ways for your friends' names and online state and writes only counts and field names to the log. Nothing is shown in the app and no friend's name or id is logged.
1.1.29 (build 529): Wording of the Steam-not-reachable message (test build).

Changed

  • The launch screen's message when Steam has not signed in now just asks you to check your internet connection.
1.1.28 (build 528): The launch screen says when Steam cannot be reached (test build).

Added

  • When a Steam game's launch is stuck because Steam has not signed in (usually a network that blocks Steam), the launch screen now says so after a short wait (about 12 seconds when Porthole's own connection to Steam also failed, otherwise about 40) and suggests your VPN, cellular data or another Wi-Fi. Before, it just kept saying it was waiting. The wait is also written to the log.
1.1.27 (build 527): Workshop downloads survive closing the app (test build).

Fixed

  • Workshop items that were waiting or downloading when the app closed (the app closes itself after a game, and you may force-quit it) are remembered and start again the next time the app opens. In build 1.1.26 the waiting list was only picked up once a Workshop screen was opened.
